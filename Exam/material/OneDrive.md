## Instructions: Upload Your Recording to OneDrive

Please follow the steps below to create a folder in OneDrive, upload your recording, and share it with me.

### Step 1: Open OneDrive

1. Log in to your school Microsoft 365 account.
2. Open **OneDrive**.
3. Make sure you are using your **school OneDrive account**.

### Step 2: Create a Folder

1. Click **New**.
2. Select **Folder**.
3. Give the folder a clear name using your **name and group number**.

For example:

`Matti_gr_11`

4. Click **Create**.

### Step 3: Upload Your Recording

1. Open the folder you created.
2. Click **Upload**.
3. Select **Files**.
4. Select your recording from your computer.
5. Wait until the upload is completely finished.

Make sure the recording appears inside the folder and can be opened.

### Step 4: Share the Folder and Submit the Link to OMA

1. Go back to your OneDrive and locate the folder containing your recording.
2. Right-click the folder or click the **three dots (...)** next to the folder.
3. Select **Share**.
4. Enter my email address (`sami.bencheikh@metropolia.fi`).
5. Make sure I have permission to **view** the folder.
6. Click **Send** to share the folder with me.
7. After sharing the folder, click **Share** again or open the sharing options for the folder.
8. Select **Copy link** to copy the link to the folder.
9. Open the assignment in **OMA**.
10. Paste the copied OneDrive folder link into your submission.
11. Submit the assignment in OMA.

Before submitting, make sure that:

* Your recording is inside the folder.
* The recording can be opened and played.
* The folder has been shared with me.
* The correct folder link has been submitted to OMA.

**If you are unable to upload the recording due to permission or account issues, please email me. I will then provide you with a direct upload folder.**

**Important:** Please use your **school OneDrive account**. Do not submit the recording as an attachment.
